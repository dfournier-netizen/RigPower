How Builds Work

Each home-run length is solved to the least total footage ≥ the run, breaking ties by fewest cables. Example: a 150′ run → 100′ + 50′. A 75′ run → 50′ + 25′ (exact) rather than a single 100′.

How Auto-Cable Works

For each circuit (power) and universe (data), fixtures are ordered into an open chain starting from the fixture nearest the homerun origin, then walking nearest-neighbor to the far end — no loops. Each gap becomes one jumper, its 3D distance rounded up to the nearest stock length (5/10/15/25/50/100′) with 10% overage or +1′ minimum. The homerun origin is the nearest placed device from the Devices tab.
Add device Devices appear in the 3D Plot and anchor homeruns. Set X/Y/Z to place one; the nearest placed device becomes the cable origin.
